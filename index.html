How the Exchange Works

A simple, fair, and cooperative way to share skills within your community without spending a dime.

1. Offer a Skill

List what you're good at. Everyone has something valuable to share.

2. Help a Member

Connect with a neighbor who needs your expertise and complete the task.

3. Earn Credits

For every hour you give, you earn one Time Credit in your account.

4. Spend Credits

Use your earned credits to get help from someone else in the network.
