How Time Banking Works

Discover how our community cooperative system allows you to trade your skills, earn time credits, and get the help you need without spending money.

The Magic of Time Credits

One hour always equals one hour. Simple and fair.

1

Earn Credits

For every hour you spend helping another member, one Time Credit is added to your account balance automatically.

2

Bank Your Time

Your earned credits stay in your account safely until you need them. They never expire and hold their value.

3

Spend Credits

Use your credits to request services from other members. Pay one credit per hour of service you receive.
