Redefining the value of time.

We believe that everyone has something valuable to offer, and that community is built when we recognize and share those gifts without the barrier of money.

How it all began

It started with a simple observation: people in our local neighborhood had incredible skills, but many couldn't afford to pay traditional market rates for services they needed. At the same time, people wanted to help others but didn't know how.

We built TimeBank to bridge that gap. By creating a system where one hour of service equals one Time Credit, regardless of the task, we created an egalitarian economy that values human connection over financial transaction.

Today, we are a thriving cooperative of thousands of members who rely on each other to learn, grow, and maintain our lives.

Our Core Values

Reciprocity

Giving and receiving are equally important. We thrive on mutual support.

Trust

Our community is built on accountability, transparency, and safety.

Inclusion

Everyone has value. All skills are respected equally.

Community

We are stronger together. Connections matter more than transactions.

Generosity

We give our time freely, knowing the community supports us back.

How we create impact

  • Economic Resilience: Members save thousands of dollars annually by exchanging services instead of paying cash.
  • Social Cohesion: 85% of our members report feeling significantly more connected to their local neighborhood.
  • Skill Development: We provide a safe space for people to practice new skills, from public speaking to web design.
